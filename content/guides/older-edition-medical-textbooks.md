## Why the subject matters

A new edition does not always mean new knowledge. Sometimes the changes are layout, images and reordered chapters. In other subjects, a few years is enough for treatment recommendations to change. The question to ask is how fast the underlying content moves.

## Subject-by-subject guide

| Subject | Older edition? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Anatomy | Usually fine | Human anatomy does not change; new editions mostly update images and clinical boxes |
| Histology and embryology | Usually fine | Core content is stable |
| Physiology | Often fine | Core mechanisms are stable; one edition back is rarely a problem |
| Biochemistry | Often fine | Core pathways are stable; newer editions add molecular medicine topics |
| Pathology | Use caution | Disease classifications and molecular findings are revised |
| Pharmacology | Buy current | New drugs, withdrawn drugs and changed recommendations |
| Clinical medicine and guidelines | Buy current | Management recommendations are updated regularly |
| Exam review books | Buy current | They follow the current exam content and are revised frequently |

## Three checks before buying an older edition

1. **Page and chapter numbers.** If your lecturers assign readings by page, an older edition will not match. Chapter titles usually still do.
2. **Online access codes.** Question banks and e-book access bundled with a new copy are often single-use and may not work with a used copy.
3. **How far back.** One edition back is usually a small difference. Several editions back is a different book.

## Where the savings are biggest

The large reference texts cost the most and change the least between editions, so that is where an older copy saves the most money. Examples include a full anatomy text such as {{< amazon q="Moore Clinically Oriented Anatomy" text="Moore's Clinically Oriented Anatomy" >}} or a physiology reference such as {{< amazon q="Guyton and Hall Textbook of Medical Physiology" text="Guyton and Hall" >}}.

For pharmacology, a current copy of a standard text such as {{< amazon q="Katzung Basic and Clinical Pharmacology" text="Katzung's Basic and Clinical Pharmacology" >}} is the safer choice.

## A note on borrowing

Before buying any edition, check your library. Many schools provide the standard texts as e-books, and you may only need to buy the one or two books you use every day.
