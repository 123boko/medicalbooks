Med Student Bookshelf is an independent site that helps medical students choose textbooks.

## How the guides are written

Each guide compares the standard books for one subject. Comparisons are based on what each book covers, how it is organized and which kind of course or exam it suits. The guides do not rank books by commission, and they regularly recommend borrowing a book or buying an older edition when that is the better choice.

Prices and edition numbers change often, so the guides do not quote them. Check the current edition on the retailer's page before you buy.

## How the site is funded

The site earns a commission when readers buy through its Amazon links. See the [affiliate disclosure]({{< relref "disclosure.md" >}}).

## Not medical advice

Everything here is about choosing study materials. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
