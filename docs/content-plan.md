Rule for every article: one question, answered in the first paragraph, with facts checked against the publisher or the exam body.

## Not targeted

- Phrases ending in "pdf" or "free download": these searchers want pirated copies.
- "for mbbs", "neet pg", Indian-market titles: different books and a different store; revisit only if the site adds an India section.
- Nursing and pharmacy textbooks: a separate audience.
